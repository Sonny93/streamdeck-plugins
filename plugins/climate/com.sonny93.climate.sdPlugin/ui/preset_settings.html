About presets

With a mode, the key turns the unit on in that mode. Without one, it only sets the temperature and the fan rate in the current mode and leaves the power as it is. Dry and Fan have no temperature, and the temperature must fit the mode: 18 to 32 in Cool, 10 to 30 in Heat, 18 to 30 in Auto, in steps of 0.5. A preset that cannot be applied shows as unavailable.

About the address

The address of the unit on your local network, shared by every action of the plugin and kept on this computer only. It is checked before it is saved. Give the unit a fixed address in your router (DHCP reservation) so it does not change.
