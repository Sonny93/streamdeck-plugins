How the dial works

Turn to choose a temperature: the strip shows it in color under "PUSH TO SET" and nothing is sent yet. Push the dial briefly to apply it, or hold it for about half a second to cancel it. A choice left alone is cancelled after 10 seconds.

Save address

About the address

The address of the unit on your local network, shared by every action of the plugin and kept on this computer only. It is checked before it is saved. Give the unit a fixed address in your router (DHCP reservation) so it does not change.
